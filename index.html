ℹ️ About Bagalkot Heritage Tourism

🏛️ Our Heritage Journey

Bagalkot Heritage Tourism is a digital tourism application created to bring the historical, cultural, architectural and natural attractions of Bagalkot district into one easy-to-use platform. Visitors can explore important destinations, view photographs, read information about individual attractions, locate places on Google Maps and plan their visit using the registration and booking features.


The application includes Badami, Aihole, Pattadakal, Mahakuta, Kudalasangama, Almatti, Chikkasangama, Banashankari, Muchkandi and Guledgudd. Together these destinations represent ancient architecture, temples, rock-cut caves, sacred places, rivers, dams, lakes, traditional crafts and local culture.
